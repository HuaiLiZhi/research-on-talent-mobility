## Figure legend

Expansion in recorded researcher mobility and geographic coverage. a,c, Annual international (a) and domestic intercity (c) mobility events. Red curves show observed totals; blue curves hold mean events per recorded route at its initial-year level. b,d, Percentages of destination countries (b) and domestic destination cities (d) connected to at least the indicated number of sources. Domestic percentages give equal weight to 76 countries. Colours distinguish 1998, 2007, 2016 and 2025. e, All 12,218 unique domestic destination cities recorded during 1998–2025 in the same 76 countries. Each equal-sized dot represents one city. Cities are grouped by country and coloured by first recorded domestic inflow. Within each group, earlier records are placed near the centre and later records near the edge. The four year labels denote entry cohorts: 1998, 1999–2007, 2008–2016 and 2017–2025, respectively. Coloured dashed outlines enclose continental groups. Country positions indicate grouping only.
